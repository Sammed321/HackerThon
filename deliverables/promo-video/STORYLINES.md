# Storylines — SmritiGraph demo film

Subject: SmritiGraph, a cultural context decoder / knowledge graph / open-standards export hub for
Karnataka's endangered audiovisual heritage (Hacktoberfest Hack Day 2026, Track 2).
One thing: **preserve the *understanding* of the reel, not just the file — in open standards, forever.**
One viewer doing one task: a heritage researcher opens a digitized 1924 reel and needs it decoded.

## A · "The reel decodes itself" (BUILT — recommendation)
- **Device & form:** the object-to-object chain. Every room is entered through the real thing on
  screen that opens it: a highlighted term opens its Context Card, the card's "View in Heritage
  Constellation" chip becomes the graph, a citation chip jumps the camera back to the transcript,
  the IIIF manifest block scrolls itself.
- **Viewer sees/feels:** the archive answering its own puzzle; each real artifact unlocks the next,
  ending in "The museum outlives us." Pride, then reverence.
- **First two seconds:** full Media Studio plate, camera resting on the synchronized transcript —
  the gold annotation hits glowing inside a 1924 field announcer's sentence.
- **Beats:** hook (hits on the transcript) → tension (a reel that says only "source: AIR Dharwad";
  words you can't parse) → reveal (SmritiGraph — The Living Archive) → proof verbs (click a term →
  card, card → graph, ask → cited answer, chip → verify, hub → manifest, form → ready-to-merge PR)
  → payoff (Presentation 3.0 manifest · "The museum outlives us.") → claim ("heritage ends where
  context begins") → end card (github.com/smritigraph/smritigraph).
- **Look:** the app's own editoral-daylight UI as world 1; a black archival film loop (the app's own
  dark player field) as world 2; gold, rationed. **Sound:** calm 92-bpm heritage underscore from a
  faint tape hum, opening on the reveal; clicks/pops on real actions, whoosh on world entries, one
  riser into the payoff.

## B · "One question to the archive" (not built)
Device: the cursor as a researcher's hand — one continuous take that walks the rooms without a cut,
misleading question first ("What's a charkha?"), every screen a stop along one desk.

## C · "1924 → 1974 → 1984: three tapes, one thread" (not built)
Device: A number lands. Form: match cuts — the same small object (a timestamp) holds while the reel
around it swaps across decades, each reel teaching the graph one more year.
